## 📌 MEME POSTING RULES

1. **Be FUNNY first** — If it's not funny, nobody shares it
2. **Keep it simple** — If you need to explain the joke, it's too complex
3. **Use trending formats** — Check what memes are trending NOW and adapt them
4. **Post at peak times** — 9AM, 12PM, and 6PM EST get the most engagement
5. **Always include hashtags** — #PLAYDOGS #NFT #CryptoMemes #NFTMemes
6. **Engage with replies** — Reply to every comment on your memes
7. **Repost your best memes** — If a meme did well, post it again 2 weeks later
8. **Let the community make memes** — Their memes will be even better than yours
