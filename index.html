Questions

FAQ

What are PlayDogs?
PlayDogs is a collection of 10,000 unique, procedurally generated 8-bit pixel art dog NFTs on the Ethereum blockchain. Each dog is a one-of-a-kind digital collectible with a unique combination of breed, skin type, background, and accessories.
How much does a PlayDog cost?
Each PlayDog is listed at 5 ETH. We use OpenSea Lazy Minting, which means the NFT is minted on-chain when you purchase it. The buyer pays the Ethereum gas fee at the time of purchase.
How do I buy a PlayDog?
You need a MetaMask wallet with enough ETH. Visit our OpenSea collection page, pick the dog you love, and click "Buy Now". The NFT will be minted directly to your wallet.
What blockchain are PlayDogs on?
PlayDogs live on the Ethereum mainnet as ERC-721 tokens. The smart contract also implements EIP-2981 for on-chain royalty enforcement (4% on secondary sales).
How can I verify authenticity?
We publish a SHA-256 Provenance Hash that covers all 10,000 images. Each image was individually hashed, concatenated in order, and hashed again. This immutable hash proves no images were swapped or modified after generation.
What do I get as a holder?
Holders get exclusive Discord channels, early access to future drops, eligibility for the breeding system, merchandise with your specific dog, and voting rights in the PlayDogs DAO when it launches.
